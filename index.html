Chai, jo milan ke liye bani

Chai & Charcha 2024 mein is waja se bana tha ke hum chahte the ek aisi jagah jo subah ki doodh patti aur raat ki karak chai — dono ka maqaam ho. Yahan chai sirf drink nahi, sohbat hai: dost ki takraar, dholki ki hasi, aur ek cup jo thode waqt ke liye poori duniya ko mil jaye.

Har pot ek restaurateur grandmother ki tarkeeb par ban-ti hai — chini kam, karak zyada, aur mohabbat woh ingredient jo kabhi recipe mein likha nahi milta.

  • ☕ 3-tareeqa karak, doodh patti aur kahwa
  • 🍽️ Fresh samose, bun kebab aur desi bites
  • ✨ Cozy corners jo charcha ke liye bane hain
